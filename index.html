Smart TV privacy

Your TV knows what you're watching. Even from HDMI.

Good to know

Will anything stop working?

No. Apps, streaming, HDMI, voice remote (unless you turn voice off) all keep working. You lose "personalized" recommendations and some interactive extras. On Roku, "More Ways to Watch" suggestions go away.

Will I still see ads?

Yes, some. Ad rows on the home screen are part of the platform. These steps stop the TV from tracking what you watch and from targeting ads at you. On LG you can also hide home screen promotions.

Does it delete what was already collected?

No. Roku, for example, says data collected while the setting was on is kept. Turning it off stops new collection from now on.

I can't find the setting

Menu names change by year, region and firmware. ACR may be called Viewing Information Services (Samsung, Hisense), Live Plus (LG), Samba Interactive TV (Sony), Smart TV Experience (Roku), Viewing Data (Vizio). In some regions it's off by default, so the option may be missing. Check the alternative path under each step.

Why trust this page?

It runs entirely in your browser, has no tracking and collects nothing. Steps come from Google, Apple, Sony, LG, Roku and Xiaomi support docs, TV manuals and published reporting (sources below). Where a maker publishes no path, the page says to check the alternative. Code is open on GitHub.
